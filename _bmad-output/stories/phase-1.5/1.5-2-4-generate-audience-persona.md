## User Story

**As a** user,
**I want** the system to generate a detailed audience persona from my inputs,
**So that** I can visualize who I'm creating content for.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: AI Synthesis
- [ ] Workers AI generates detailed persona including:
  - Name and avatar placeholder
  - Demographics summary
  - Top 3 pain points
  - Top 3 aspirations
  - Preferred platforms

### AC2: PersonaCard Display
- [ ] Display in PersonaCard component
- [ ] Store in `audience_personas` table with `client_id`
