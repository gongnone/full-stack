# Story 1.5-7-9: Automated Nudge Emails

**Epic:** 1.5-7 - Agency Owner Dashboard
**Priority:** P1
**Status:** review
**Effort:** 4-6 hours
**Created:** 2025-12-30

---

## User Story

**As an** agency owner,
**I want** stuck clients to receive automatic reminder emails,
**So that** I don't manually track and nudge every client.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: 3-Day Nudge
- [ ] Friendly reminder: "Your brand story is waiting..."
- [ ] Update `clients.last_nudge_sent_at`

### AC2: 7-Day Nudge
- [ ] CC agency owner
- [ ] Slightly more urgent tone

### AC3: 14-Day Nudge
- [ ] Alert agency: "[Client] may need a phone call"
- [ ] Offer to schedule a call

### AC4: Completion Cancellation
- [ ] On status = "complete", cancel all scheduled nudges

### AC5: Opt-Out
- [ ] "Stop reminding me" in email footer
- [ ] Set `clients.nudge_opt_out` = true
- [ ] Notify agency of opt-out

## Reverse Engineering Finding

Marcus shouldn't manually check daily - automation handles the nagging.
