# Story 1.5-7-3: Invite New Clients via Email

**Epic:** 1.5-7 - Agency Owner Dashboard
**Priority:** P0
**Status:** review
**Effort:** 4-6 hours
**Created:** 2025-12-30

## User Story

**As an** agency owner,
**I want** to invite new clients via email link,
**So that** I can onboard them easily.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: Invite Form
- [ ] Client name, email, optional welcome message

### AC2: Email Send
- [ ] Unique signup link in email
- [ ] Client record created with `status` = "invited"
- [ ] Appears in pending invites list
