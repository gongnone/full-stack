# Story 1.5-7-8: Proxy BrandDNA Completion

**Epic:** 1.5-7 - Agency Owner Dashboard
**Priority:** P1
**Status:** review
**Effort:** 6-8 hours
**Created:** 2025-12-30

---

## User Story

**As an** agency owner,
**I want** to complete BrandDNA on behalf of clients who won't engage,
**So that** client onboarding doesn't stall indefinitely.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: 14-Day Trigger
- [ ] "Complete for Client" button after 14 days inactive
- [ ] Warning: "This uses your inputs, not client's voice"

### AC2: Client Consent
- [ ] Email: "Agency wants to help. Allow? [Yes] [No]"
- [ ] Block proxy until consent or 48h timeout

### AC3: Proxy Session
- [ ] Mark inputs as "Submitted by Agency"
- [ ] Record consent: "explicit" or "implicit (timeout)"
- [ ] Notify client: "Agency started your profile"

### AC4: Client Review
- [ ] Banner: "Your agency created this draft. Review and personalize."
- [ ] Approve, edit, or re-record
- [ ] Mark "Client Verified" on save

## Round Table Finding

Marcus concerned clients might hate proxy - require consent first.
