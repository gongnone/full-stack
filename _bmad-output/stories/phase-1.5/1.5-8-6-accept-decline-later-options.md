# Story 1.5-8-6: Accept/Decline/Later Options

**Epic:** 1.5-8 - Testimonial Collection
**Priority:** P0
**Status:** review
**Effort:** 2-3 hours
**Created:** 2025-12-30

---

## User Story

**As a** user,
**I want** to accept, decline, or defer the testimonial request,
**So that** I control my participation.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: Accept
- [ ] "Sure, I'll share" activates VideoRecorder

### AC2: Decline
- [ ] "No thanks" sets `testimonials.declined_at`
- [ ] Don't ask again for 30 days

### AC3: Later
- [ ] "Ask me later" reminds after next batch approval
