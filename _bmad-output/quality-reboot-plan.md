# Quality Reboot Plan — "Quality In, Quality Out"

## The Problem (Honest Assessment)

What the Creator agent receives today to generate content:

1. **Brand DNA** — Voice markers, banned words, tone profile (extracted by Llama 8b from a single voice recording)
2. **Source Content** — Pillar title + whatever `hub_sources` has (often empty or a thin transcript)
3. **Hooks** — Empty array or generic suggestions
4. **Platform specs** — Character limits and format instructions
5. **Research Report** — Industry/niche detection, framework fit scores (also generated by Llama 8b)

**That's it.** We're asking an AI to write publishable social media content with:
- No examples of what good looks like for this brand
- No knowledge of the audience beyond a generic "target customer" string
- No awareness of what's trending in the industry
- No competitor content to differentiate from
- No past performance data
- No specific stories, case studies, or data points to reference

A human copywriter given this brief would send it back. We're expecting Llama 70b to do better.

## What Real Content Creation Requires

### Layer 1: WHO (Brand Identity) — We have ~30% of this
**What we capture now:**
- Voice recording → Whisper transcription → Llama extracts markers
- Optional content upload (PDF/text)

**What we need:**
- [ ] **Example Content Library** — 5-10 pieces of the client's BEST existing content (social posts, blogs, emails). Not for the AI to summarize — for it to PATTERN MATCH against. "Write like this."
- [ ] **Anti-Examples** — Content the client hates. "Never write like this." Equally important.
- [ ] **Audience Personas** — Who reads this? What do they care about? What language do they use? Not demographics — psychographics.
- [ ] **Brand Stance Registry** — Hot takes, opinions, things this brand believes that others don't. (We have `BrandStance` in the DO schema but it's never populated meaningfully.)
- [ ] **Vocabulary Bank** — Industry jargon they use, jargon they avoid, proprietary terms, hashtags they own.

### Layer 2: WHAT (Content Fuel) — We have ~10% of this
**What we capture now:**
- Pillar titles from strategy synthesis
- Source content (usually empty or thin)

**What we need:**
- [ ] **Story Bank** — Client's real stories, anecdotes, case studies, data points. AI without stories writes platitudes.
- [ ] **Content Seeds** — Specific ideas, angles, data points for each pillar. Not "Leadership" but "That time I fired my best performer because they were toxic, and revenue went UP."
- [ ] **Industry Intelligence** — What's happening in their space? What are competitors posting? What's trending? (RSS feeds, news monitoring)
- [ ] **Client's Own Data** — Revenue numbers they can share, client outcomes, survey results. Specificity = credibility.
- [ ] **Swipe File** — Content from OTHER creators the client admires. Style references, not content to copy.

### Layer 3: HOW (Context & Timing) — We have ~5% of this
**What we capture now:**
- Platform-specific formatting rules (good)
- Hook patterns from Vectorize (ok but thin)

**What we need:**
- [ ] **Engagement Data Feedback** — What worked? What flopped? Adjust. (Epic 11 exists but isn't connected to generation)
- [ ] **Content Calendar Context** — What was posted yesterday? Last week? Avoid repetition.
- [ ] **Trending Topics** — What's hot in the industry RIGHT NOW? Timely content outperforms evergreen 3:1 on social.
- [ ] **Platform Algorithm Intelligence** — LinkedIn loves carousels right now. TikTok rewards story hooks. Instagram pushes Reels. These shift quarterly.

## The New Ingestion Pipeline

### Phase 1: Onboarding Overhaul (Required for quality output)

```
CURRENT FLOW:
  Voice Recording → Whisper → Llama → Brand DNA → Generate Content
  
NEW FLOW:
  1. Voice Recording → Whisper → Brand DNA (keep)
  2. Content Upload (5-10 best pieces) → Extract patterns, voice, structure → Example Library
  3. Brief Questionnaire:
     - "Who is your ideal audience?" (free text)
     - "What are your 3 strongest opinions in your field?" (stances)
     - "Name 3 creators you admire" (swipe references)  
     - "Paste your best-performing social post ever" (gold standard)
     - "What should your content NEVER sound like?" (anti-examples)
  4. URL Scrape (optional):
     - Website → Extract about page, mission, services
     - LinkedIn profile → Extract positioning
     - Existing social feeds → Extract top posts by engagement
```

### Phase 2: Content Fuel Engine (Ongoing ingestion)

```
CONTINUOUS INPUTS:
  1. Story Bank (client adds stories/anecdotes via chat or form)
  2. RSS/News Monitor (industry feeds → daily digest of potential content angles)
  3. Engagement Feedback (what performed → weight similar patterns)
  4. Client Edits (what they change → learn preferences)
  5. Swipe File (save interesting content from anywhere)
```

### Phase 3: Smart Generation (Better prompts with better inputs)

```
CREATOR AGENT receives:
  - Brand DNA (voice, stances, vocabulary) 
  - 3 example posts (closest matches to this pillar/platform)
  - 1 anti-example 
  - Specific content seed (not just pillar title)
  - Recent trending topics in their industry
  - What was posted in the last 7 days (avoid repetition)
  - Best performing hook patterns from their data
  - Audience persona context
```

## Implementation Priority

### Sprint A: Foundation (3 days) — Gets us from 30% → 70% quality input
1. **Example Content Ingestion** — Upload 5+ existing posts during onboarding, store as `content_examples` with platform/type/performance tags
2. **Brief Questionnaire** — Add structured questions to onboarding flow (audience, stances, admired creators, gold standard post, anti-examples)
3. **URL Scraper** — Fetch and extract from website/LinkedIn (web_fetch style, store in `hub_sources`)
4. **Creator Prompt v2** — Inject real examples + stances + audience into prompt. This alone will transform output quality.

### Sprint B: Content Fuel (3 days) — Gets us from 70% → 85%
5. **Story Bank** — Simple CRUD for client to add stories/anecdotes/data points tagged by pillar
6. **Content Seeds** — When generating a hub, require at least 1 specific angle/seed per pillar (not just a title)
7. **Calendar Awareness** — Pass last 7 days of generated content to Creator to prevent repetition
8. **Edit Pattern Learning** — Track what users change, surface patterns to Creator

### Sprint C: Intelligence (4 days) — Gets us from 85% → 95%
9. **Industry News Monitor** — RSS feed ingestion + AI summarizer for relevant angles
10. **Engagement → Generation Loop** — Top-performing patterns weighted in hook selection
11. **Competitive Monitor** — Track competitor social accounts, identify gaps
12. **Trending Hook Injection** — Real-time trending topics as optional content seeds

### Model Strategy (Day 1)
- **Creator**: Move to external API (Claude Haiku or GPT-4o-mini) — $0.001-0.003/spoke
- **Critics**: Keep Llama 8b on Workers AI (scoring is fine for small models)
- **Research**: Move to external API (needs reasoning quality)
- Add `model_provider` abstraction layer for easy swapping

### Pipeline Simplification (Day 1)
- **Kill G4** (voice alignment) — Roll into Creator prompt with real examples instead
- **Kill G5** (platform compliance) — JS character count check, not an LLM call
- **Kill G6** (visual clichés) — Merge into Visual Strategist prompt
- **Keep G2** (hook quality) — Real quality signal
- **Keep G7** (engagement prediction) — Gets better with data over time
- **New flow**: Creator → Score (G2 + G7 parallel) → Polish if needed → Done
- 3 LLM calls instead of 7. Every spoke produces output. Scores = quality indicator, not gate.

## What to Monitor for Great Ideas

### Automated Monitors
1. **Industry RSS Feeds** — Client selects 3-5 relevant sources during onboarding
2. **Competitor Social Feeds** — Track top 3 competitors' recent posts
3. **Trending Hashtags** — Platform-specific trending topics in their niche
4. **Content Performance Patterns** — Which hooks, formats, topics drive engagement
5. **Audience Questions** — What is the audience asking? (Reddit, Quora, Twitter replies)

### Client-Driven Inputs
1. **"Something happened today"** — Quick story/insight capture (mobile-friendly)
2. **"I saw this and loved it"** — Swipe file addition
3. **"Our client just..."** — Case study/testimonial capture
4. **"Hot take:"** — Stance/opinion capture

### AI-Driven Discovery
1. **Gap Analysis** — "Your competitors talk about X, you don't"
2. **Pattern Recognition** — "Your audience engages most with contrarian hooks on Tuesdays"
3. **Content Recycling** — "This post from 3 months ago could be refreshed with new data"

## Kill List

Things to remove or stop doing:

1. ❌ **creative_conflict status** — Everything produces output. Period.
2. ❌ **G4, G5, G6 as separate LLM calls** — Wasteful. Roll into Creator prompt or JS checks.
3. ❌ **7-gate sequential pipeline** — 3-phase parallel pipeline instead.
4. ❌ **Hiding failed content from users** — Show everything, sort by quality score.
5. ❌ **Generating without source material** — Refuse to generate if inputs are below minimum threshold.
6. ❌ **Llama 70b for creative writing** — External API for Creator (keep Llama for critics).
7. ❌ **Abstract pillar titles as content seeds** — Require specific angles/stories.

## The Core Insight

**The product isn't a content GENERATOR. It's a content SYSTEM.**

Generators are a commodity (ChatGPT, Jasper, Copy.ai — everyone has one). 

A system that:
- Deeply understands YOUR brand (not just voice markers — stories, stances, audience)
- Continuously learns from YOUR engagement data
- Monitors YOUR industry for timely angles
- Gets BETTER over time as more data flows in

That's a product worth paying for. That's the moat.
