# Story 1.5-2-10: Single Audience Prompt (Simplified Flow)

**Epic:** 1.5-2 - BrandDNA Agent - Audience & Strategy Discovery
**Priority:** P1
**Status:** ready-for-dev
**Effort:** 3-4 hours
**Created:** 2025-12-30

---

## User Story

**As a** user,
**I want** to describe my audience in one sentence and have AI expand it,
**So that** I can skip lengthy questionnaires while still getting a useful persona.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: Path Selection
- [ ] ButtonChoice: "Quick Description" or "Guided Questions"

### AC2: One-Sentence Input
- [ ] Single text field: "Who is your ideal audience?"
- [ ] Placeholder: "Tech founders scaling from $1M to $10M ARR"

### AC3: AI Expansion
- [ ] Workers AI expands to full persona:
  - Inferred demographics
  - Inferred psychographics
  - Inferred content preferences
- [ ] Display in PersonaCard

### AC4: Review Options
- [ ] Approve as-is, edit, or switch to "Guided Questions"
- [ ] Note: "AI-Expanded - refine for better results"
- [ ] Skip Stories 2.1-2.4, proceed to Story 2.6

## Occam's Razor Note

Most users know their audience - one sentence is often enough.
