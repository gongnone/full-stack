## User Story

**I want** to upload long-form content as my Source of Truth,
**So that** the system can fracture it into multiple pieces.

- [ ] Create hub record in D1

### AC3: Text Extraction

- [ ] Audio/video via Whisper
- [ ] Store `source_type` and `source_url`
